Privacy

The short version: everything stays on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

What Plutus stores

Your budgets, expenses, categories, the money you have lent and borrowed, repayments, savings goals and your settings. All of it is written to your browser's local storage on the device you are using.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Plutus has no server, no account and no sign in. It never sends your financial data anywhere, because there is nothing on the other end to send it to. The app is a set of static files, and once it has loaded it works with no connection at all.

What is collected about you

  • No analytics.
  • No advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No cookies used to identify you.
  • No third party services, fonts or scripts. Everything the app needs ships with it.

What this means in practice

Your data is as private as the device it sits on, and just as fragile. Clearing your browser data, uninstalling the app, or wiping the phone will delete everything, and there is no copy anywhere for anyone to restore. If your records matter to you, use the export in Settings and keep the file somewhere safe.

Children

Plutus is a personal budgeting tool. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if an optional backup or sync is added later, this page will say so before the feature ships, and anything of that kind will be something you switch on rather than something switched on for you.
